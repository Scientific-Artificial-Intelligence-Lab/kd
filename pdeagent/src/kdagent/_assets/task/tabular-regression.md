## Task: tabular regression

Fit target column {target} from feature columns {features}.
All columns: {fields}. Axes: {axes}.
The discover, pysr and pysindy tabular modes derive their inputs from these
columns; `terms` is not accepted. Numeric constants inside regression
expressions are allowed. Discover and PySR report one whole-expression term
and its outer coefficient; PySINDy reports selected polynomial terms and
coefficients. PySINDy's library is polynomial, so low residual on a
nonpolynomial target may be a polynomial approximation.
